Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

Who this is about

Zenoeats operates the ordering software that restaurants use to take pickup and delivery orders. When you place an order you are dealing with two organisations at once: the restaurant, which prepares your food and receives your payment, and Zenoeats, which runs the site the order is placed through.

Payment is taken by the restaurant, on the restaurant’s own payment account. The restaurant — not Zenoeats — is the merchant of record for your order, and refunds and card disputes are handled by them.

What is collected, and why

Your name, email address and phone number
Required on every order. The restaurant needs to know whose order it is and how to reach you if something is wrong with it, and your email is where the confirmation goes.
An address
Required at checkout. For a delivery it is where the food goes, and it is measured against the restaurant’s delivery area to work out the fee. For a pickup order it is kept as part of your contact details.
What you ordered
The items, options and any note you attached, along with the prices at the time. Kept as a record of the order.
A pickup code
Generated for you to collect the order with. It is stored encrypted and is not readable by Zenoeats staff.
Your account, if you create one
Your sign-in details are held by Clerk, our identity provider, not by Zenoeats. We keep your email, name, phone number, address and any items you have saved as favourites, so you do not have to type them again.
Ordering without an account
You can order as a guest. A cookie in your browser is then the only thing linking you to that order — it is how you reach the order page and your pickup code. Nothing about you is verified.
A driver’s location, during a delivery
While your delivery is on the road, the driver’s position is shown on your order page. It is held only while the delivery is in progress, and each position is discarded within 10 minutes of being reported.
Technical records
Server logs, and error reports when something breaks. Error reports are stripped of personal information before they are sent: pickup codes, payment identifiers, email addresses and order notes are removed.

Cookies

Zenoeats sets a small number of cookies, all of them necessary for the site to work. There is no advertising or analytics tracking.

  • Your session. If you sign in, a cookie keeps you signed in. If you order as a guest, a cookie is what identifies your order to you afterwards.
  • Sign-in. Clerk sets cookies needed to sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • Payment. Stripe sets cookies needed to process your card payment and to detect fraud.

Who your information is shared with

Only with the companies needed to take your order and get it to you. None of them is given your information for their own marketing.

The restaurant you ordered from
Receives your name, phone number, email, the order itself, and the delivery address where there is one. They receive nothing about orders you placed with any other restaurant.
Stripe — payments
Your card details are entered directly into Stripe and never reach Zenoeats’ servers. Stripe receives the amount, the order reference and your email address, which it uses to screen the payment for fraud. Stripe does not email you a receipt; the emails about your order come from Zenoeats. Because the restaurant is the merchant of record, the payment is on the restaurant’s Stripe account.
Clerk — accounts and sign-in
Holds your password and runs sign-in with Google, Apple or Facebook if you choose one. Zenoeats never sees your password.
SendGrid (Twilio) — email
Sends the emails about your orders and your account: the order confirmation and each update after it, refunds, account notices, and, where it is set up, the codes you are sent to sign up or reset a password. It receives your email address and the content of each email. They never contain your pickup code.
Google — addresses and delivery
An address you enter for delivery is sent to Google to turn it into a location, so the distance and fee can be worked out. The resulting coordinates are cached for up to 30 days; what is kept on the order is the distance and the fee, not the coordinates. The address suggestions at checkout, and the map on your order page, are provided by Google Maps and load from Google, which receives your IP address and, for suggestions, what you type into the address field.
Sentry — error reporting
Receives reports when something on the site fails, with personal information removed as described above.

How long it is kept

  • Orders are kept as a business and tax record for as long as the restaurant is required to keep them.
  • Guest records that never became an order are removed automatically after about 45 days.
  • Account details are kept until you delete your account.
  • Delivery coordinates are cached for up to 30 days. A driver’s position is discarded within 10 minutes of being reported.
  • Messages from our payment and sign-in providers about you are stripped of your name, email address, phone number and address within an hour of being dealt with. What remains, such as a payment’s amount and reference, is kept for a year.
  • Backups, encrypted, are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted automatically. They are used only to restore the service after a failure.

Your choices

You can see and change your name, phone number and address, and see your past orders, on your profile page while signed in.

You can ask for your account and personal information to be deleted. How to do that, and what happens to your past orders, is set out on the data deletion page.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights over your information — to get a copy of it, to correct it, or to object to how it is used. Write to the address above.

Security

Each restaurant’s data is isolated from every other restaurant’s inside the database itself, not merely by the application asking politely. Pickup codes are encrypted. Card details never touch Zenoeats’ servers. Connections are encrypted in transit.

No system is perfectly secure, and this policy is not a warranty that one is.

Children

Zenoeats is not intended for children, and accounts are not knowingly created for them.

Changes
