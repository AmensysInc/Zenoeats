Refunds & Cancellations

Last updated 29 September 2026

The short version

The restaurant you ordered from takes the payment and issues any refund. Zenoeats does not hold your money and cannot refund it for them. Contact the restaurant first.

Before you have paid

Nothing is charged until you complete payment. If you leave checkout, the order is discarded automatically after about 30 minutes and no money moves.

After you have paid

Food is prepared to order and usually starts being made as soon as the payment clears, so a paid order generally cannot be cancelled.

If you need to try, phone the restaurant straight away. If they have not started your food they may agree to cancel and refund it. That is their decision.

If something is wrong with your order

Contact the restaurant. They can put right:

  • food that is missing, wrong, or not what was described;
  • food that arrived cold, late, or not at all;
  • an order they could not fulfil after taking payment.

The restaurant’s phone number is on your order page and in every email about your order. For a pickup order, its address is shown at checkout and in the email telling you the order is ready.

How a refund reaches you

Refunds go back to the card you paid with. They cannot be sent anywhere else. Once the restaurant issues one, it is credited to your original payment method within 10 to 14 business days.

Your order page here shows the refund and its amount once the restaurant issues it.

If you cannot reach the restaurant

If the restaurant does not respond, email PLACEHOLDER@example.com with your order number. Zenoeats can contact them on your behalf and make sure the complaint reaches someone. Zenoeats cannot issue the refund itself.

Card disputes

You can dispute a charge with your bank. The dispute goes to the restaurant, since the payment was taken on the restaurant’s account and they are the merchant of record. Talking to the restaurant first is almost always faster.
