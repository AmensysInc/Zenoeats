What Zenoeats is

Zenoeats is ordering software. Restaurants use it to publish a menu and take orders; you use it to place one. Zenoeats does not cook, sell or deliver food.

Your order is a contract between you and the restaurant. The restaurant sets its prices, decides what it sells, prepares the food, takes the payment on its own payment account and, where it offers delivery, makes the delivery. The restaurant is the merchant of record.

Ordering

  • You can order with an account or as a guest. Either way you must give a name, a phone number, an email address and an address.
  • The details you give must be your own and must be correct. A wrong phone number or address is the most common reason an order goes wrong, and it is not something the restaurant can fix for you.
  • Prices shown include the taxes the restaurant is required to charge. The price is always calculated by the server when you order, so what you are charged is what the restaurant has actually set.
  • An order is not accepted until the payment is confirmed. Until then nothing has been charged and nothing is being prepared.
  • A restaurant may refuse or cancel an order — because it has run out of something, is closing, or cannot deliver to your address.

Paying

Payment is by card, through Stripe, on the restaurant’s account. Card details are entered directly into Stripe and never reach Zenoeats.

Zenoeats may take a fee from the restaurant for each order. That fee is between Zenoeats and the restaurant and is not added to your bill.

Collecting and delivery

For a pickup order you will be given a code. Show it at the counter; it is what tells the restaurant the order is yours. Do not share it.

For a delivery, the restaurant delivers within the area it has set. Any time shown is an estimate and not a promise.

Refunds and cancellations

Once a restaurant has started preparing your food an order generally cannot be cancelled. Refunds are decided and issued by the restaurant. The full position is on the refunds and cancellations page.

Food, allergies and intolerances

Menu descriptions, ingredients and allergen information come from the restaurant. Zenoeats does not prepare the food, does not inspect kitchens and cannot verify what is in a dish.

If you have a food allergy or intolerance, contact the restaurant directly before ordering. Food is prepared in kitchens that handle allergens, and cross-contact cannot be ruled out.

Your account

  • Keep your sign-in details to yourself.
  • Do not use the service to place false orders, to interfere with it, or to try to reach information that is not yours.
  • An account may be suspended for any of the above. You can close your account at any time — see data deletion.

Availability

Zenoeats is provided as it is. It is not guaranteed to be uninterrupted or error-free, and it may be taken down for maintenance.

Where responsibility sits

Complaints about food — its quality, its safety, whether it matched the description, whether it arrived — are for the restaurant that sold it.

Zenoeats is responsible for the ordering software itself. Nothing in these terms removes any right you have that cannot be removed by agreement, including rights under consumer protection law.
